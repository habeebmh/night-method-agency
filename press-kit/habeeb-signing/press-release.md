## Night Method Agency Signs Habeeb

**June 2026** - Night Method Agency has signed Habeeb, a Dallas-based DJ and producer whose public catalog sits in the space between house, afro-house, remix culture, mashups, DJ tools, and club-ready edits.

The signing brings Habeeb into Night Method's boutique management and strategy office for select artists, producers, and culture-facing projects. Night Method will support Habeeb across management, release planning, live strategy, partnerships, creative direction, and the operational layer around the next phase of the project.

Habeeb's public-facing music activity includes edit-pack releases and a club-minded production language built for DJs, rooms, and high-momentum sets. His work connects nightlife utility with a clear artist signal: functional enough for the booth, distinctive enough to travel beyond it.

Night Method's approach is intentionally selective and practical. For Habeeb, that means building the run around stronger release rhythm, clearer positioning, live opportunities, and the right partnership conversations without overexposing the project before the work is ready.

“Habeeb has the kind of club instinct that turns loose attention into movement,” said Night Method Agency. “The goal now is to build around that signal with discipline: better infrastructure, sharper rollouts, and the right rooms.”

Habeeb is now represented by Night Method Agency. Press, booking, partnership, and collaboration inquiries can be directed to bookings@nightmethodagency.com.

## About Habeeb

Habeeb is a Dallas-based DJ and producer active across house, afro-house, remix, mashup, DJ-tool, and club-edit spaces. His public releases and edit packs reflect a high-energy, room-first approach shaped for nightlife, DJs, and culture-forward audiences.

## About Night Method Agency

Night Method Agency is a boutique management and strategy office for select artists, producers, and culture-facing projects. The agency supports artist management, development, release strategy, live strategy, campaigns, partnerships, and special projects with a discreet, artist-first approach.
