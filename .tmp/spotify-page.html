About

17,619 monthly listeners
Habeeb is an independent artist and DJ from Dallas making tech house, UK speed garage, and bassline-inspired music focused on energy, escapism, and making people feel good. Before releasing original tracks, he built a following through mashups and remixes of popular songs that picked up thousands of downloads online. His independent release Money on My Mind received a strong reception and helped introduce his sound to a wider audience. In 2026, he performed at Breakaway Dallas and continues playing clubs and after hours events around the city. At the core of it all, he just loves creating music that helps people let go and enjoy the moment.
